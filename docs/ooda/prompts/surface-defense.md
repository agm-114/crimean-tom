Respond to a reported or visually observed surface attack. Use the current briefing, the latest applicable engagement orders in `missionMessages`, and the player's contacts. Pick one owned ship able to respond promptly.

- Engage a surface contact actively firing at your force or a ship you are protecting. A clear `observedGunfire` pair identifies the firing contact and victim. Act on that evidence while the contact remains live unless the overall situation calls for holding fire.
- A credible first-hand report naming or describing the attacker can justify engaging before you see the shots yourself. Match the report to a specific live contact by visible class, position relative to the victim, timing, and other observations. A gunboat reported firing on an escorted tanker and a uniquely matching fast attack craft near that tanker are enough to respond. A vague report or several equally plausible attackers weakens the case for fire.
- Apply any specific restriction from the briefing or later command messages. An order to avoid escalation calls for restraint toward uninvolved contacts. It still allows proportionate defensive fire against the specific attacker when applicable orders permit it. Do not wait for a new operational plan when there is already enough evidence and authority to act.
- Prefer a ship already positioned to respond. Avoid duplicate fire on a target already engaged. The native order will try guns first and select another available weapon if necessary.

Return `action` as `Engage` or `Hold`, with a short `reason` that names the evidence. Assess the order separately with `roeAssessment` (`Consistent`, `Unclear`, or `PossibleViolation`) and explain it in `roeReason`. You can choose to engage despite that warning when protecting the threatened ship warrants the risk. The host records the warning and immediately asks for a revised plan. For `Engage`, return an owned `shipId`, the live contact's `targetId`, the `victimId`, and the evidence. Set `messageIndex` to the matching report's index for `AttackReport`. Use `None` when evidence is unconfirmed. For `Hold`, use `None` for `evidence` and null for all IDs and `messageIndex`.
